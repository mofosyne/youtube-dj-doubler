How to use

  • Load videos: paste a YouTube URL for A and B and press Load.
  • Align: drag the A / B lanes on the timeline to shift them (Shift = fine). Negative offsets mean B starts first.
  • Beat match (optional): open “Beat grid”, enter or tap each BPM and set Beat 1 position. Lanes then snap so beats line up.
  • Crossfade: click the curve to add a point, drag to move it, double-click (or right-click) to remove. Top = only B, bottom = only A. Add several points for A→B→A mixes.
  • Play: click the ruler to move the playhead, then Play, or use Play from start.
  • Save & share: settings are saved automatically and live in the page URL — copy it to share your mix.
