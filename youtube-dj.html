How to use

  • Example: load an example mix to see markers, beat grids and a crossfade curve already set up.
  • Load videos: paste a YouTube URL for A and B and press Load. Each video is pre-loaded (briefly started muted, then paused) and Play stays locked until they are ready, so both start together.
  • Start / end flags: every lane has a flag at each end. Drag the left one to choose where the clip starts and the right one to choose where it stops; the music stays lined up and the flags snap to that clip’s own beats and markers (Shift = free).
  • Start / end of the mix: the cyan flags in the ruler mark where the whole mix starts and ends. Drag them (they snap to beats and markers; Shift = free). Play is limited to that range: it always starts at the start flag (or the playhead if it is inside) and stops at the end flag.
  • Zoom & move: the timeline starts zoomed to show both whole clips. Use − / + (or Ctrl + scroll) to zoom, Shift + scroll or the strip under the timeline to move, and Fit to see everything again.
  • Align: drag the A / B lanes on the timeline to shift them (Shift = fine). Negative offsets mean B starts first.
  • Beat match (optional): open “Beat grid”, enter or tap each BPM and set Beat 1 position. Lanes then snap so beats line up.
  • Crossfade: click the curve to add a point, drag to move it, double-click (or right-click) to remove. Top = only B, bottom = only A. Add several points for A→B→A mixes.
  • Markers: pause a video where something important happens (vocals, drop) and press “Mark here”. A flag appears on its lane; click it to jump the playhead there and press “Start here” to cut the clip to start there (it stays lined up with the other clip), or “Sync to…” and then click a marker on the other clip to line the two up. Double-click deletes a marker. Dragging a lane also snaps a marker on A to a marker on B.
  • Speed: change a video’s speed, or set both BPMs and press “Match to A / B” to make the tempos equal. YouTube only allows certain speeds, so the match can be slightly off, and speeding up also raises the pitch.
  • Play: click the ruler to move the playhead, then Play, or use Play from start. Space plays and pauses (click outside the video first, because a clicked YouTube player keeps the keyboard for itself).
  • Save & share: settings are saved automatically and live in the page URL — copy it to share your mix.
