🎵 YouTube DJ Doubler

Create amazing DJ-style mixes with YouTube videos. Choose between two modes: Basic for simple mixing, or Sample for advanced sample-based DJing.

Basic Mode

Simple video mixing with crossfade

  • Load two YouTube videos
  • Adjust start times and offsets
  • Control volume for each video
  • Crossfade between videos
  • Sync playback
Easy

Sample Mode

Advanced sample-based DJing

  • Record samples from videos
  • 16 sample pads
  • BPM and tempo control
  • Loop and sync options
  • Export/import samples
Advanced

Note: YouTube videos must be publicly available and not age-restricted.
